About Us

A hotel rooted in warmth, comfort, and a little bit of nature.

Opened in 1975 in Nairobi,Kinoo, we set out to create a place that feels less like a stop along the way and more like somewhere you're glad to end up. We kept what was already good about the building and added what a great stay needs: comfortable beds, quiet rooms, and a team that remembers your name.
