Rooms

Our rooms are very spacious with one,two and three bedrooms. There are rooms with two beds in one bedroom and are available upon request. All the rooms have amazing views.

Luxury spa

We offer a use of or luxury spa one time after paying for any room. The second or even third time has to be paid in separate amount from the one paid for the roooms. You are guranteed to feel pampered and relaxed after just one sesssion in our spa.

Play ground

Kids get engaged throughout their stay at our hotel by playing in our well equiped play ground. Parents do not need to watch them everytime as there is security and a medical personell within the ground just incase of an emergency.

Conference Space

A conference room is available upon request. The space can hold upto 150 people and is perfect for weddings, work meetings and even debutanes. Whatever function you want to hold,we can provide the services together with designers and caterers.
